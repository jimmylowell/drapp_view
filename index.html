Denver Aerials

Every year of the Denver Regional Aerial Photography Project at one address, read from DRCOG's public archive in your browser. How it works

Where this comes from

The tile index and the archive tiles are the DRCOG Regional Data Catalog, licensed CC BY 3.0. 2018 to 2024 stream from DRCOG's mosaics hosted by Sanborn Geospatial; 2004 to 2016 are read straight from the raw GeoTIFFs on DRCOG's S3 archive, a few hundred bytes at a time, one image row per request, so nobody has to download a 446 MB file. 2002 (JPEG 2000) and 2008 (MrSID) are offered as downloads. The base map is OpenStreetMap. Addresses are placed by the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder, falling back to Nominatim.

Every year is rendered on the same ground square (Colorado State Plane Central) and then warped onto the map, so features line up year to year. Use ← and → to step through loaded years. Source and notes on GitHub. Part of myjimmycloud.com.

Rendered squares stay in your browser so a refresh at the same address is instant; nothing leaves your machine. Clear the cache.
