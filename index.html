Denver Aerials

Every two years since 2002 the Denver Regional Aerial Photography Project (DRAPP) has photographed the whole metro area from the air, down to 3 inches a pixel. The pictures are public. This page is a way to look at them: move around, step through the years, put two side by side, save what you find.

How the years load

2018 to 2026 come as map tiles from DRCOG's mosaics, hosted by Sanborn; 2026 is the interim mosaic. Nothing is fetched while the map is moving. When it stops, the tiles on screen arrive at ordinary screen detail.

2004 to 2016 are not served as a map anywhere for most of the region. Picking one reads just the ground on screen out of DRCOG's raw archive files and leaves it on the map. After that, moving somewhere new reads nothing until you press Load here, which says what it will cost first. 2002 and 2008 are in file formats a browser cannot open.

Inside Arapahoe County the county's own map service has every flight from 1998 to 2026, so every year there is instant.

Before DRAPP there was no regional programme. The older years come from whoever has scans online and cover only their own ground: the Denver Public Library's 1933 aerial survey of central Denver, Boulder County (1977 and 2000), South Metro Fire Rescue's scans of its district by decade from the 1950s to the 1990s, and CDOT's scans of Golden (1930), Boulder (1948) and Englewood–Littleton (1953). The timeline only shows the years that exist where you are looking.

Your data

On a phone or a high-density screen, HD on the timeline sharpens the picture to the screen's own pixels once the map has rested, zoomed in. It is about three times the data; tap it to turn it off.

Everything the page fetches is kept in this browser so the same ground costs nothing twice. Nothing is sent anywhere.
