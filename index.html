Denver Aerials

Every year of the Denver Regional Aerial Photography Project at one address. DRCOG publishes the imagery free, but as multi-hundred-megabyte tiles named by grid cell. This page finds the right tiles and reads only the pixels around your address, in your browser, with no server in between.

Where this comes from

The tile index and the archive tiles are the DRCOG Regional Data Catalog, licensed CC BY 3.0. 2018 to 2024 stream from DRCOG's mosaics hosted by Sanborn Geospatial; 2004 to 2022 can also be read straight from the raw GeoTIFFs on DRCOG's S3 archive, a few hundred bytes at a time. 2002 (JPEG 2000) and 2008 (MrSID) are offered as downloads. Addresses are placed by the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder, falling back to Nominatim © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Every square is the same patch of ground on the same pixel grid (Colorado State Plane Central), so features line up year to year. Older years load slowly on purpose: the browser asks the archive for one image row at a time so nobody has to download a 446 MB file. Source and notes on GitHub. Part of myjimmycloud.com.

Rendered squares stay in your browser so a refresh at the same address is instant; nothing leaves your machine. Clear the cache.
